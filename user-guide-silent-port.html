System Overview

Silent Port is a web platform for managing your company's payroll comprehensively.

The system is organized into modules that cover the entire payroll cycle, from staff registration to the generation and signing of payslips.

System module map

What does this MVP include?

AreaFeatures
AuthenticationRegistration, login, password recovery, email verification, multiple sessions
CompaniesCreate and manage multiple companies, switch between them, configure settings
TeamInvite members, assign roles (Owner, Admin, Manager, Member), transfer ownership
EmployeesIndividual and bulk creation (Excel), editing, deactivation, search and filters
ContractsBase salary, work schedule, concepts assigned per employee
TimeAttendance (clock in/out), overtime (50% / 100%), holiday calendar
ConceptsEarnings and deductions with fixed, percentage, hourly or manual calculation
PayrollPeriods, automated runs, payslips, line editing, closing, CSV/PDF export
SettlementAutomatic calculation for dismissal or resignation (bonus, vacation, severance)
Self-ServiceEmployees can view and sign their payslips

Registration & Login

How to create your account and access the system.

Login screen

Create a new account

  1. Go to the registration page from the "Create account" link on the login screen.
  2. Fill in the fields (see table below).
FieldDescription
NameYour full name (optional)
Email addressThis will be your username for logging in
PasswordMinimum 8 characters, with uppercase, lowercase and a number
  1. Click "Create account". You will receive a confirmation email.
  2. Open the email and click the verification link.

Log in

  1. Enter your email address and password.
  2. Click "Log In".
  3. You will be redirected to the company list.

Recover password

  1. On the login screen, click "Forgot my password".
  2. Enter your email and click "Send link".
  3. Check your inbox and click the link you received.
  4. Enter your new password (twice) and click "Reset".
!
Email not verified?If you try to log in without verifying your email, you will see a message with the option to resend the confirmation email.

Team Members

Invite users and manage access permissions for the company.

View members

The table shows all users with access to the company, their role and join date.

Add a member

Requires ADMIN role or higher.

  1. Click "Add member".
  2. Enter the user ID (the user must have a Silent Port account).
  3. Select the role: Admin, Manager or Member.
  4. Click "Add".

Change role

Use the dropdown in the Role column to change it. The change takes effect immediately.

Remove a member

From the options menu, select "Remove". A confirmation will be requested.

Transfer ownership

Only the OWNER can transfer ownership. This action is irreversible.

!
ImportantThe owner cannot be removed, and a user cannot remove themselves.

Employee list with search, filters and table

  2. Fill in the required fields (see table below).

Contracts

Employment conditions for each employee.

Contracts are managed from each employee's profile. Only one can be active at a time.

Create a contract

Requires ADMIN role or higher.

  1. Go to the employee detail view.
  2. Click "New Contract".
  3. Fill in the details (see table below).
FieldDescription
Start dateWhen the contract begins
Base salaryMonthly amount
Employment typeType of employment
  1. Click "Create".

Work schedule

Each contract can have a schedule (Monday to Sunday) with hours per day. It is used to calculate attendance and overtime.

Contract concepts

Assign payroll concepts (earnings/deductions) to the contract. They will be applied automatically when generating payroll.

  1. Expand the employee's active contract.
  2. In "Concepts", search and select the ones that apply.
  3. To remove a concept, click the delete button next to it.
i
AutomaticConcepts assigned to the contract are calculated in each payroll run without manual intervention.

Payroll Concepts

Define the earnings and deductions that make up the payslip.

Each concept represents an income (earning) or discount (deduction) on the payslip. They are organized into two tabs: Earnings and Deductions.

Calculation types

TypeHow it worksExample
FixedAmount that is always applied the sameBonus of Gs. 500,000
PercentageCalculated as % of basic or gross salarySocial Security 9%
HourlyMultiplied by hours workedGs. 25,000/hour
ManualEntered manually in each runAdvance deduction

Create a concept

Requires ADMIN role or higher.

  1. Click "New Concept".
  2. Fill in the form (see table below).
FieldDescription
CodeShort identifier, e.g.: SAL_BASE, SS_9
NameDescription of the concept
CategoryEarning or Deduction
Calculation typeFixed, Percentage, Hourly or Manual
ValueAmount, percentage or rate depending on the type
OrderPosition in which it appears on the payslip
  1. Click "Save".

You can activate/deactivate concepts without deleting them. An inactive concept is not included in new runs.

12

Payroll

Periods, runs and payslip generation.

The payroll module has three levels: Periods → Runs → Payslips.

Complete payroll processing flow

Periods

A period defines the date range (e.g., March 1 to 31). Types: Monthly, Biweekly, Weekly or Custom.

  1. Click "New Period".
  2. Select the type, name (optional), start and end dates.
  3. Click "Create".

Run statuses

StatusMeaning
DraftCreated but not processed
ProcessingThe system is calculating
CompletedPayslips ready for review
ClosedFinalized, cannot be modified

Process payroll step by step

  1. Create run: from a period, click "New Run".
  2. Calculate: click "Calculate". The system processes earnings and deductions for each active employee.
  3. Review: verify the payslips. Click on an employee to see the breakdown.
  4. Adjust: if you need to modify an amount (manual concepts), use the edit icon.
  5. Close: when everything is correct, click "Close Run" (irreversible).
  6. Export: download as CSV or PDF (individual or all).

Run detail with summary and payslips

Signatures

Administrators see a progress bar indicating how many employees have signed their payslips.

!
Closed runOnce closed, you will not be able to modify any payslip. Review all amounts before closing.
13

Employee Settlement

Automatic calculation of final settlement for dismissal or resignation.

Settlement result with concept breakdown

Generate a settlement

Requires ADMIN role or higher.

  1. Go to the employee detail view.
  2. Click "Generate Settlement".
  3. Select the reason: Dismissal (includes severance) or Resignation (prorated amounts only).
  4. Specify the termination date.
  5. Click "Confirm".

What does the system calculate?

ConceptDismissalResignation
Prorated monthly salaryYesYes
Prorated bonusYesYes
Prorated vacationYesYes
Notice period severanceYesNo
Seniority severanceYesNo
!
IrreversibleWhen generating the settlement, the employee is marked as inactive and their contract is automatically closed.

Configure rules

Calculation rules are customized from Settings → Settlement (ADMIN): notice period days, severance per year (default 15 days) and vacation according to seniority.

My Payslips

View and sign payslips (employee view).

i
Members onlyThis section is visible only to users with the MEMBER role. Higher roles access payslips from the Payroll module.

View payslips

You will see a list with the period, net salary and signature status (Signed / Pending). Click to view the breakdown.

Payslip detail

Shows three cards (Gross, Deductions, Net) and the list of earnings and deductions with their amounts.

Sign a payslip

  1. Open the pending payslip.
  2. Optionally write a comment.
  3. Click "Sign Payslip".
  4. The status will change to Signed with the date and time. This cannot be undone.

Future Implementations

Modules and features planned for upcoming versions of Silent Port.

i
RoadmapThe following features are in planning. Priorities and scope may be adjusted according to user needs.

17.1 Unions and Collective Bargaining Agreements

Priority: High

Unions

  • CRUD for unions (name, code, representative, contact, union dues)
  • Employee assignment to unions at the contract level
  • Union dues as an automatic deduction concept (percentage or fixed amount)
  • Union dashboard: number of members, total costs
  • Affiliation/disaffiliation history per employee

Collective Bargaining Agreements

  • CRUD for agreements (name, code, validity from/to, associated union)
  • Salary scales by agreement (categories and amounts)
  • Concepts associated with the agreement (agreement bonus, seniority, attendance)
  • Agreement and category assignment at the employee contract level
  • Agreement expiration alerts and version history

Professional Categories

  • Category definition within each agreement
  • Base salary by category
  • Promotion and recategorization rules

AI-Powered Agreement Upload

  • Upload PDF/image of the collective bargaining agreement
  • Automatic AI extraction: categories, salary scales, supplements, validity
  • Human review: the system suggests and the user confirms/corrects before saving
  • Key clause detection (working hours, vacation, seniority bonus)
  • Agreement comparator: differences between previous and new version

17.2 Intelligent Employee Analysis (AI)

Priority: High

At-Risk Employee Detection

  • Risk score per employee based on: absenteeism, tardiness, excessive overtime, sanctions
  • Automatic alerts when an employee exceeds configurable thresholds
  • Dashboard with employee ranking by risk level
  • Trends: evolution of employee behavior

Predictive Analysis

  • Turnover prediction: probability of an employee resigning based on patterns
  • Attendance anomaly detection (abrupt behavior changes)
  • Automatic recommendations based on historical data

AI Reports

  • Monthly executive summary generated by AI in natural language
  • Work climate analysis based on objective metrics
  • Automatic insights by department and team

17.3 Reports and Dashboards

Priority: High

  • Labor cost charts by period (bar, line)
  • Month-over-month comparisons
  • Salary distribution by department, union and agreement
  • PDF report export
  • Dashboard KPIs: headcount, average cost, turnover

17.4 Notifications

Priority: Medium

  • Contract expiration (30/15/7 days in advance)
  • Payroll periods pending closure
  • Agreements about to expire
  • Pending approvals
  • Channel: in-app + email (configurable per user)

17.5 Audit / Logs

Priority: Medium

  • Change history for employees, contracts and payrolls
  • Record of who did what and when
  • Filters by entity, user and date
  • Log export

17.6 Multi-Level Approvals

Priority: Medium

  • Configurable flow: MANAGER → ADMIN → OWNER
  • Payroll approval before closing
  • Employee onboarding/offboarding approval
  • Pending approvals panel

17.7 Documents

Priority: Medium

  • File upload per employee (contracts, IDs, certificates)
  • Document categorization
  • Expiration dates with automatic alerts
  • Inline viewing (PDF and images)

17.8 Employee Portal (Self-Service)

Priority: Medium

  • Personal payslip view
  • Vacation and leave requests
  • Personal data updates
  • Digital payslip signing
  • Employment certificate downloads

17.9 Organization Chart

  • Hierarchical company visualization

17.10 Performance Reviews

  • Objectives and KPIs per employee

17.11 Finance and Accounting
